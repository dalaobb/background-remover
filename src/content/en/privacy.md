## 🔒 Privacy Policy

Your images belong to you. That's the core promise of BgGone.

## 🛡️ No Uploads

All background removal happens locally in your browser using on-device AI. Your images never leave your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

## 🍪 No Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or any analytics that follows you across the web. This website runs entirely in your browser.

## 📊 Basic Analytics

We use a privacy-respecting analytics tool to understand aggregate page traffic. It does not collect your personal data or track you across websites.

## ✉️ Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, please contact us via the website at [bgg.one](https://bgg.one).
